Frequently Asked Questions

Is Flutter really as good as native?expand_more
For the vast majority of apps, Flutter delivers a native-quality experience. Its custom rendering engine (Impeller) achieves consistent 60-120fps across both platforms. Apps like Google Pay, BMW App, eBay Motors, and Hamilton Musical are all built with Flutter and indistinguishable from native in daily use.
How much cheaper is cross-platform vs native?expand_more
Cross-platform typically saves 30-50% in development cost and 20-40% in timeline compared to building separate native iOS and Android apps. You maintain one codebase, one QA cycle, and one release process — the savings compound over the product lifetime.
Which should I choose — Flutter or React Native?expand_more
Choose Flutter for design-heavy apps requiring pixel-perfect custom UI, smooth animations, or future web/desktop expansion. Choose React Native if your team already knows JavaScript, you need to leverage an existing web codebase, or you prioritise the largest plugin ecosystem. We provide a free technical recommendation based on your specific requirements.
Can cross-platform apps access device hardware like camera and GPS?expand_more
Yes. Both Flutter and React Native have mature plugin ecosystems covering camera, GPS, biometrics, Bluetooth, NFC, notifications, and more. For highly specialized hardware features, we write thin native modules that integrate seamlessly with the cross-platform codebase.
