Why Native?

Native Apps Outperform Every Time

speed

Maximum Performance

Native code runs directly on the device's processor without abstraction layers — delivering 60 fps animations and instant response times that users feel.

hardware

Full Hardware Access

Deep integration with camera, GPS, biometrics, NFC, and Bluetooth — capabilities that cross-platform solutions struggle to match.

security

Superior Security

Native apps leverage platform-level security — iOS Keychain, Android Keystore, Face ID, and fingerprint authentication without third-party bridges.

star

Platform-Native UX

Users expect iOS apps to feel like iOS and Android apps to feel like Android. Native development delivers the platform-appropriate UX that drives 5-star ratings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a native iOS or Android app?expand_more
A typical MVP takes 6–12 weeks depending on complexity. Simple apps with 5–8 screens can be done in 4–6 weeks, while complex apps with real-time features, payment integrations, and custom APIs take 12–20 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline after the discovery workshop.
Do you build for both iOS and Android?expand_more
Yes. We have dedicated iOS (Swift/SwiftUI) and Android (Kotlin/Jetpack Compose) engineers on staff. We can build both simultaneously for faster time-to-market, or start with one platform based on your target audience and budget.
What is your pricing model?expand_more
We work on both fixed-price (for well-defined projects) and time-and-material (for evolving requirements) models. Native app projects typically start from ₹3–5 lakhs for a basic MVP and scale based on feature complexity. Contact us for a detailed estimate.
Do you provide post-launch maintenance?expand_more
Yes. We offer flexible maintenance packages covering bug fixes, OS version compatibility updates, performance monitoring, and feature enhancements. Our SLA guarantees critical bug fixes within 24 hours.
