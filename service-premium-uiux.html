Frequently Asked Questions

Do you design for both iOS and Android?expand_more
Yes. We design platform-appropriate versions for both iOS (following Apple Human Interface Guidelines) and Android (following Material Design 3). For cross-platform apps built in Flutter or React Native, we create a single adaptive design system that renders correctly on both platforms.
What deliverables do we receive?expand_more
You receive: Figma files with all screens and component libraries, an interactive prototype for user testing, exported assets (icons, illustrations, images) in all required resolutions, a design system documentation file, and a dev-handoff Figma file with annotations, spacing specs, and colour/typography tokens.
How long does UI/UX design take?expand_more
Wireframes are typically delivered within 5–7 business days. Full high-fidelity UI design for a 20-30 screen app takes 3–4 weeks. Complex apps with 50+ screens, custom illustrations, and full design systems take 6–8 weeks. We work in parallel with development where possible to compress overall timelines.
Can you redesign an existing app?expand_more
Absolutely. We conduct a UX audit of your existing app to identify friction points, usability issues, and drop-off patterns. We then propose a redesign strategy — either a full overhaul or a phased improvement approach — ensuring continuity for existing users while delivering a dramatically improved experience.
